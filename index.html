Why Tanglish Breaks Standard NLP

70% of movie comments are positive. Naive classifiers optimize for majority accuracy, burying genuine criticism. Four linguistic bottlenecks worsen this:

1Spelling Variation

No standard Tanglish orthography: padam appears as padham, paadam, or batham. Tokenizers treat each as a new word, creating extreme sparsity.

2Agglutinative Suffixes

Suffixes (-la, -oda, -ku) stick to English roots (trailer-la, hero-oda). 85.8% of words in social feeds are Out-of-Vocabulary in transliteration dictionaries.

3Emoji Sarcasm

Cleaning scripts discard non-ASCII emojis, yet they carry the emotion: “padam mass 🤮” is sarcastic, but stripped of emojis it looks like glowing praise.

4Mid-Sentence Reversals

“Sema hype aana climax mokka”: Bag-of-words ignores clause turns, so the positive words overpower the negative clause following aana (but).

How ConG-MoE v2 Works

ConG-MoE v2 replaces resource-heavy transformers with a fast, discourse-guided 3-head mixture of experts:

Head 1 · Statistical Classifier

Class-weighted logistic regression over 35,000 sub-word n-grams (10k words + 25k word-bounded chars). Penalizes minority errors by up to 12.5×.

Head 2 · Conjunction Pivot Monitor

Watches polarity changes across pivot conjunctions (aana, aanal, irundhalum, but). Opposing sentiments on either side vote Mixed.

Head 3 · Affective Emoji Resolver

Catches emotional dissonance between text praise and disgusted emojis (e.g., praise alongside 🤮 or 👎).
